# Revisics

> **Revisics studies not merely what a system can do, but how what it does changes what can still be done.**

**Revisics** is a proposed scientific field organized around the study of **evolving transformability**: how transformations of a system alter the transformations, interventions, revisions, or counterfactual changes that remain possible afterward.

The field begins from a simple distinction:

\[
\text{state dynamics: } x_t \rightarrow x_{t+1}
\]

versus

\[
\text{revisic dynamics: } \mathcal T_t(x_t) \rightarrow \mathcal T_{t+1}(x_{t+1}),
\]

where \(\mathcal T_t(x)\) denotes a declared set or structure of transformations reachable from state \(x\) at time \(t\).

The characteristic question is:

\[
\boxed{\text{How does changing a system change what can still be changed?}}
\]

## Status

Revisics is a **proposed scientific field organized around a research object**. The existence of the field does **not** imply that its proposed laws, invariants, metrics, geometries, or mathematical structures have already been established.

Two boundaries are constitutional:

\[
\boxed{\text{field definition} \neq \text{empirical confirmation}}
\]

\[
\boxed{\text{research lineage} \neq \text{retroactive evidence}}
\]

Earlier projects may motivate, constrain, or supply antecedent results for Revisics without being retroactively reclassified as experiments of a theory that did not yet exist.

## Scope

Revisics is broader than corrigibility, correction, or AI safety. Those are possible domains within the field.

Candidate revisic questions include:

- How does learning alter what can later be learned or unlearned?
- How does representation change which future distinctions remain expressible?
- How does compression change which future operations remain reconstructible?
- How does control alter future controllability?
- How does accumulated causal leverage alter the interventions capable of reducing or redirecting that leverage?
- How do institutions, organisms, machines, or other adaptive systems reshape their own future transformation spaces?

A project belongs to Revisics scientifically only when this recursive transformation-of-transformability question is part of the object being studied. Tools used to study such questions are infrastructure; neighboring work remains neighboring work.

## Constitutional principle

Revisics does not begin by assuming a universal scalar of revisability or corrigibility. Sets, graphs, topologies, response maps, tangent operators, equivalence relations, costs, delays, and other structures may all be useful in particular assays. None is constitutional merely because it is mathematically convenient.

Every stronger object must be earned by a declared model, theorem, or experiment.

## Practitioner identity

The field is **Revisics**. A practitioner is a **revisicist**. The adjective is **revisic**.

Potential subfields may eventually include revisic geometry, revisic topology, revisodynamics, revisic information theory, and applied Revisics. These names are organizational possibilities, not claims that mature subdisciplines already exist.

## Founding boundary

This repository begins constitutionally rather than by declaring a scientific victory. Canonical experiments may be added only with their own prospective contracts, provenance, controls, and claim ceilings.

The founding research object is therefore deliberately modest:

\[
\boxed{\textbf{the changing structure of transformations that remain possible after transformation.}}
\]
